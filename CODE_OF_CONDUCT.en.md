# Code of Conduct

[Português do Brasil](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [Español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md) | [English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md)

## Our commitment

As participants, contributors, and maintainers, we pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, appearance, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, race, religion, identity, or sexual orientation.

We are committed to acting in an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy manner.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive community include:

- demonstrating empathy and respect for different experiences and viewpoints;
- giving and receiving constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing, and learning from the experience;
- prioritizing what is best for the community and the project;
- respecting other people's privacy, safety, and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwanted sexual attention;
- intimidation, stalking, insults, or derogatory comments;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing private information without explicit permission;
- spam, manipulation, fraud, or encouragement to violate program rules;
- any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or educational setting.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may edit, reject, or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with these rules.

Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict the participation of people who engage in inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual publicly represents the project or its community.

## Reporting

Behavior concerns can be reported privately to the maintainer through the contact information available on the [@tkusal](https://github.com/tkusal) profile.

All reports will be reviewed with respect for the safety and privacy of everyone involved. Retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report will not be tolerated.

## Enforcement guidelines

Actions may include private guidance, a warning, content removal, temporary restrictions, or permanent exclusion from the community, depending on the severity, frequency, and impact of the behavior.
